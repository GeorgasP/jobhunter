# 🎯 JobHunter — Product Vision

## The Problem

```
Job application πραγματικότητα στο 2026:
  • Μέσος εργαζόμενος εφαρμόζει σε 50-100 jobs για 1 offer
  • Κάθε εφαρμογή = 30-60 λεπτά
  • Total time investment: 50-100 ώρες δουλειάς
  • 75% rejection χωρίς feedback
  • Mental burnout = γιατί 70% παρατάνε σε 2 μήνες
```

**The result:** Talented άνθρωποι μένουν άνεργοι ΟΧΙ επειδή δεν έχουν αξία,
αλλά επειδή το process είναι ψυχοφθόρο.

## Our Solution

            αντί για 30 ώρες.
```

## Mission Statement

**"Eliminate the soul-crushing busywork of job hunting. Let humans focus
on what matters: the interview and the job."**

## Target Users

```
PRIMARY (Year 1):
  • Tech professionals seeking remote/relocation
  • Recent graduates entering job market
  • Career changers
  • Layoff survivors (post-2024 tech layoffs)
  
SECONDARY (Year 2):
  • Non-tech professionals (sales, marketing, customer success)
  • International professionals seeking EU jobs
  • Bilingual workers (Greek, Spanish, German, French)
  
ENTERPRISE (Year 3):
  • Career coaches with 50+ clients
  • Outplacement firms
  • University career centers
```

  • Multi-language support

  • Application tracking dashboard
  • Cancel anytime

PREMIUM — €49/μήνα ($54):
  • Unlimited job matches
  • Unlimited AI cover letters
  • One-click apply για supported ATS

  • Analytics dashboard (rejection patterns, time-to-response)
  • Priority email support
  • LinkedIn outreach templates

ENTERPRISE — €499/μήνα:
  • 50 sub-accounts (για career coaches)
  • White-label option
  • Admin dashboard
  • API access
  • Dedicated support
```
