JobHunter

A browser extension that searches job boards for you, scores every posting against what you actually want, and fills in the application forms.

How it works

  • Reads your CV once and fills in the rest from it.
  • Searches twice a day across job boards and company career pages.
  • Scores each posting out of 100 — title, location, salary, freshness — and shows you why, so it is never a black box.
  • Prepares the application and fills the form. You review it and press submit.

What it does not do

Nothing leaves your browser. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Your CV and your applications are stored on your own machine and are never uploaded anywhere.
